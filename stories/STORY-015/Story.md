# STORY-015: Final quality and review loop for stem-based mastering

## User Story
As a mastering engineer, I want a final review layer that checks the real quality outcomes of the process so that I can see whether the track is clearer, more controlled, less fatiguing, and more dimensional after mastering.

## Contract
The tool must provide a final review and decision loop that uses before/after metrics, stem-level analysis, and a human-review summary to determine whether the mastered output improved in a musically meaningful way or should be rejected or tuned further.

## Scope
- In scope:
  - final before/after quality review
  - stem and bus-level reporting
  - over-processing detection
  - manual review decisions
- Out of scope:
  - pretending a metric pass is equivalent to a musical quality pass
  - hiding risky changes behind a clean report
  - shipping an output that fails quality review
