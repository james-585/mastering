# STORY-014: Stem-aware bus glue and final dynamic balance

## User Story
As a mastering engineer, I want a final bus stage that glues the mix together and keeps the emotional contour intact so the finished track feels depth-rich and controlled rather than loud and flat.

## Contract
The final bus stage consumes corrected stems and applies only the minimum glue and dynamic shaping necessary to unify the mix. It must not flatten transients or erase the musical shape of the arrangement.

## Scope
- In scope:
  - gentle bus compression / glue
  - final dynamic balancing
  - perceived depth and control
  - loudness safety at the final stage
- Out of scope:
  - over-compression as a default strategy
  - replacing all earlier stem corrections with a final blanket limiter
  - claiming to recover source quality that was never present
